\## Images



\### Background — "Cosmic Cliffs" in the Carina Nebula



The background used in the onboarding videos and app is a NIRCam image of the

"Cosmic Cliffs" in the Carina Nebula (NGC 3324), one of the first images

released by the James Webb Space Telescope in July 2022.



\- \*\*Credit:\*\* NASA, ESA, CSA, and STScI

\- \*\*Image processing:\*\* J. DePasquale (STScI)

\- \*\*Source:\*\* https://esawebb.org/images/weic2205a/

\- \*\*NASA release:\*\* https://science.nasa.gov/missions/webb/nasas-webb-reveals-cosmic-cliffs-glittering-landscape-of-star-birth/

\- \*\*Modifications:\*\* Cropped and scaled to fit the application background;

&#x20; overlaid with user interface elements.
