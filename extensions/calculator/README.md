# Calculator

Evaluate arithmetic and convert units from a compact widget. The palette shows
the same results inline as you type.

- Arithmetic: `+ - * /` (also `x` and `×`), parentheses, and a comma or a point
  as the decimal separator (`3,5*2`).
- Conversions: `<amount> <unit> in <unit>` (or `to`), for example `5 km in mi`,
  `20 °C in F`, `100 km/h in mph`, `1,5 GB in MB`, `90 min in h`. Units cover
  length, area, volume, mass, time, temperature, data, and speed.
- Continue from the last result: start with an operator (`*2`), or with `in km`
  to convert a converted result again.
- ↑ and ↓ in the input step through earlier expressions. Click the live result,
  or press Ctrl+C with no text selected, to copy it.
