# Moodist extension — licenses & attribution

This extension vendors a curated subset of ambient sounds from the open-source
[Moodist](https://github.com/remvze/moodist) project and ships small inline icons
for the Kavibay widget UI.

- Upstream: https://github.com/remvze/moodist
- License text: https://github.com/remvze/moodist/blob/main/LICENSE

## Third-party sound assets

Sounds under `sounds/` are sourced from Moodist’s public assets. Per the upstream
README, some sounds are subject to different licenses:

- **Pixabay Content License**: https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/
- **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

When redistributing or modifying this extension, retain this attribution and
respect the applicable sound licenses in addition to Moodist’s MIT terms.
