Privacy

What this site stores.

Last updated: 31 August 2026 · Applies to this website, not to the app.

The short version

This is a static page plus one small form. No cookies for tracking, no analytics, no advertising pixels, no third-party embeds — so there is nothing to consent to unless you submit the form. Fonts and the videos on this page are served from the same site; opening it does not tell a font or video host that you were here. The demos run in your browser. The only personal data that reaches us is an email address you type into the notify form yourself.

Who is responsible

Alexander Swetlow, Helene-Lange-Str. 16, 14469 Potsdam, Germany — see the Impressum. For any question about your data, write to hello@kavibay.com.

Server logs

Like every site, the host that answers requests for kavibay.com records them: IP address, time, the file requested, referrer and browser string. That happens so the page can be delivered and the service kept running — legal basis Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in a site that works. Those logs are not combined with anything else on this site and are not used to profile anyone.

The notify form

When you submit the notify form, this server emails your address to the operator. The mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail). We do not keep a copy on the web server. Legal basis is your consent, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time by writing to hello@kavibay.com — we then delete that mail.

No newsletter, no list rental, no third-party marketing — and the address is not needed for anything else on this site.

What stays on your device

The demos on this page (the Wizard, the launcher palette, the widget gallery) run locally in your browser. Anything they keep — playback position, a typed search — stays on your machine. We do not receive it.

The app is a separate matter

Kavibay itself runs on your machine. Widgets are sandboxed and only reach the network when a widget you installed asks to, and the AI widget builder talks to whichever model provider you configured with your own API key — that traffic goes from your computer to that provider and never through this site.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what data is stored about you, have it corrected or deleted, have its processing restricted, receive it in a portable form, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. You may also complain to a supervisory authority. For this site that is the Brandenburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Access to Information (LDA Brandenburg), lda.brandenburg.de.
